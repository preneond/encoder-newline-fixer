Newline Fixer ↵

Fixes newline placement in English text — paragraph breaks, line breaks before bullets and headings, and words split mid-line. The model only rewrites whitespace: the output words are guaranteed identical to the input. Runs entirely in your browser (quantized ONNX via transformers.js) — no server. Model: preneond/newlinefix-encoder.
